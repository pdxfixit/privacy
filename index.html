Arrow-Rama privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Arrow-Rama is a puzzle game for Android phones, tablets and Wear OS watches, made by the Sandberg family.

In short: Arrow-Rama collects no personal data. It has no ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing you do in the game is sent to us.

What the game keeps, and where

  • On your device. The player names and pictures you choose, your progress, scores and settings. They stay on your device.
  • On your watch. If you use the watch app, your phone and watch copy this game data to each other through Google Play services on your devices. It goes to your own watch or phone, not to us.
  • In Android's backup. If backup is on, Android includes the phone's game save in your Google account's backup, so a new phone can restore it. Google keeps that backup for you; we can't see it. The watch's data is not backed up.
  • A play log, on the device only. To help us tune the puzzles, the game notes boards and taps in a file on your device. It holds no names and no account. Each player in it is a random number the game makes up, so a board can be told apart from another player's. The game has no way to send it anywhere. Old entries delete themselves after 14 to 90 days.

Children

Arrow-Rama is made for all ages, children included. It collects no personal information from anyone, children or adults. The only name in the game is a player name you type, and it stays on your devices as described above. There are no ads, no purchases and no chat.

Permissions

The game asks for only two ordinary permissions: to vibrate (for taps you can feel) and to keep the screen awake while you play. It does not use the camera, your location or your contacts, and it has no microphone permission. On the watch you can search shapes by voice: your watch's own speech service turns what you say into text, and the game gets only the text.

The game's sounds include a few short clips our family recorded. They are sounds the game plays; the game never records you.

Deleting your data

Uninstall Arrow-Rama to delete everything it keeps on that device. To remove the phone's backup copy too, delete it in your Google account's backup settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
