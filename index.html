Arrow-Rama privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Arrow-Rama is a puzzle game for Android phones, tablets and Wear OS watches, made by the Sandberg family.

In short: Arrow-Rama has no ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases and no tracking. To help us tune the puzzles, it sends us game moves, never names. Each player can turn that off.

What the game keeps, and where

  • On your device. The player names and pictures you choose, your progress, scores and settings. They stay on your device.
  • On your watch. If you use the watch app, your phone and watch copy this game data to each other through Google Play services on your devices. It goes to your own watch or phone, not to us.
  • In Android's backup. If backup is on, Android includes the phone's game save in your Google account's backup, so a new phone can restore it. Google keeps that backup for you; we can't see it. The watch's data is not backed up.
  • Game moves, sent to us. While a player's Share play data is on (Settings, Sound & haptics; on to start with), the game sends its play log to our server every few hours: boards, taps and their timing, and settings such as font size. It holds no name, no account and no contact details. Each player is a code that changes every 90 days, so we cannot follow one person over time, and we do not keep your IP address. We use it only to tune the puzzles, looking at totals across all players. With Share play data off, that player's play is not recorded at all.

Children

Arrow-Rama is made for all ages, children included. The game moves it sends hold no name, no contact details and no lasting identifier, and we use them only to make the game work better. A grown-up can turn Share play data off for any player. The only name in the game is a player name you type, and it stays on your devices as described above. There are no ads, no purchases and no chat.

Permissions

The game asks for only two ordinary permissions: to vibrate (for taps you can feel) and to keep the screen awake while you play. It also asks for internet access, used only to send game moves while Share play data is on. It does not use the camera, your location or your contacts, and it has no microphone permission. On the watch you can search shapes by voice: your watch's own speech service turns what you say into text, and the game gets only the text.

The game's sounds include a few short clips our family recorded. They are sounds the game plays; the game never records you.

Deleting your data

Uninstall Arrow-Rama to delete everything it keeps on that device. To remove the phone's backup copy too, delete it in your Google account's backup settings. Game moves we have received cannot be traced back to you, so we cannot find yours to delete. Turning Share play data off stops any more from being sent.
