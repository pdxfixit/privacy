## Feedback you send us

- **Only when you press Send.** Settings, About, **Feedback**, after a grown-up check, sends exactly what was typed in the box, with the app's version and the date. Nothing is sent while you type, and nothing else goes with it: no random code, no name, no email address.
- **Please leave out names and contact details.** We don't ask for them, and we can't write back. If a message holds them anyway, we delete that message when we read it.
- **No IP address kept**, as for everything else.
- **Who reads it:** only us. It waits in our own Cloudflare account until our daily copy (within a day), then lives on our own computer. It is never shared, never counted in usage totals and never used to contact anyone.
- **Kept 90 days**, then deleted from every copy automatically.
