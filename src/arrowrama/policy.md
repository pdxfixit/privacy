# Arrow-Rama privacy policy

We are the Sandberg family. We make small apps: Arrow-Rama, Isopix, Noodle Pads and Orbit. They are for all ages, children included. Every one of them follows the same rules, below. Arrow-Rama is a puzzle game for Android phones, tablets and Wear OS watches.

**In short:** no ads, no tracking across apps or sites, and nothing sold. The game sends us usage data to help us make it better. It never holds a name, contact details or what you make. Each player can turn it off.

## What Arrow-Rama sends, and what it keeps

- **Sent to us**, while a player's **Share play data** is on (Settings, Sound & haptics; on to start with): the play log, every few hours. Boards, taps and their timing, and settings such as font size, with the player's random code.
- **Not sent:** player names and pictures, which stay on your devices.
- **Your own copies:** your phone and watch copy game data to each other through Google Play services. If Android backup is on, your phone's save goes into your own Google account's backup. We can't see either.
- **Permissions:** vibrate; the watch also keeps the screen awake while you play. Play builds use the internet only to send the play log. On the watch, voice search uses the watch's own speech service; the game gets only the text. The game's sounds include a few short clips our family recorded; the game never records you.

## Deleting your data

- **On your device:** uninstall the game. To remove the phone's backup copy too, delete it in your Google account's backup settings.
- **Usage data:** turn Share play data off to stop sending more. We can't tell which records are yours: the code is never shown and is linked to nothing else. It is removed after one year anyway.
- **Questions or requests:** write to [privacy@pdxfixit.com](mailto:privacy@pdxfixit.com).
